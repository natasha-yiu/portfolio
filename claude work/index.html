About

I believe the future of interaction extends beyond screens. My practice investigates how digital systems become tangible through products, space and computation—bridging UX design, immersive technologies and media architecture. Alongside commercial UX projects, I explore democratic information architecture, spatial computing and interactive visualisation through research and experimentation.
