Heureka Hackathon: Curio Museum Companion

In November 2024, we joined the DigiEduHack 2024 at Heureka, the Finnish Science Centre. In collaboration with the Finnish Fed­er­a­tion of the Visually Impaired, we addressed the challenge 'Accessible Adventures'. Our solution won honourable mention in the Access and Availability category. It is where Curio started.
